🚀 **Thrilled to announce my progress on my Laptop Price Prediction project!** 💻📊

After collecting and understanding the dataset, I focused on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and **Feature Engineering** to transform raw laptop specifications into meaningful, machine-learning-ready features.

🔍 **EDA:**
• Analyzed data distributions, feature types, and statistical summaries
• Checked for missing values and unnecessary columns
• Explored relationships between laptop specifications and price
• Identified key patterns, including the strong impact of **RAM on laptop price**

⚙️ **Feature Engineering:**
• Converted RAM and Weight into numerical features
• Separated **HDD and SSD** capacities
• Extracted **CPU and GPU brands**
• Encoded **TouchScreen and IPS** as binary features
• Simplified OS categories
• Engineered **PPI (Pixels Per Inch)** from display specifications

These steps helped turn the raw dataset into a **clean, structured, and model-ready dataset**, laying the foundation for the next stage: **Model Training & Evaluation**. 🚀
